# CECai website: working rules for Claude

This repository is the source for **cecai.ca**, the website of CECai (Change Execution Consulting AI Inc.), a people and organizational advisory firm in Greater Saint John, New Brunswick. Principal & Managing Partner: Stephanie Abbott, CPHR, CEC. Partner, AI & Transformation: Stuart MacMillan.

## How the site is built

- `src/build.py` builds the whole site into `_site/` (not committed). Run `python src/build.py`, then `python src/check_links.py`.
- Page copy lives in `src/home.py`, `src/service_content.py` (all text for the five practice pages), `src/services.py` (service page layout and the Services overview), `src/pages.py` (People & AI, How We Work, Team, bios, Collective) and `src/more.py` (Insights index, FAQ, Contact, Privacy, 404, sitemap, robots.txt, llms.txt).
- Shared header, footer, brand tokens and helpers are in `src/common.py`. Styles are in `static/assets/css/site.css`.
- **Insights articles are Markdown files** in `content/insights/`, named `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`, with YAML front matter. Copy `content/templates/article-template.md` to start one. `draft: true` keeps an article off the site. The home page shows the three newest articles automatically.
- Pushing to `main` triggers `.github/workflows/site.yml`, which builds, checks links and publishes to GitHub Pages. Pull requests are built and checked but not published.
- Domain and email stay at GoDaddy. Only the `@` A records and `www` CNAME point to GitHub Pages. Never suggest changing nameservers or MX/TXT records.

## How changes are made

1. Always work on a new branch and open a pull request. Never push to `main` directly.
2. The PR description says in plain language what changed, on which pages, and lists every new statistic with its source.
3. A human (Stuart or Stephanie) reviews and merges. Merging is what publishes.
4. Build and run the link check before opening the PR.

## Brand

- Palette: Cloud `#F0EEE9` background, Transformative Teal `#1F5C63` primary, Deep Teal `#153F44`, Moss `#5F6F52`, Brass `#B08D57` (decorative only, never for text), Cocoa `#4A3A32`, Ink `#23201D` text.
- Type: Lora for headlines and quotes, Inter for everything else.
- Tagline: **People First. Future Ready.** Hero line: **The future of work is still human.**
- Audience wording: "CEOs, owners and executive teams". Say "organization" (never "org") and "practice" or "organization" rather than "firm".
- Location: say Atlantic Canada (Saint John, Moncton, Fredericton, Halifax, Charlottetown, St. John's); Saint John appears only in the footer.
- Team photos: square 800x800 JPG + WebP on a white background in `static/assets/img/team/<first-last>.jpg`. A photo there automatically replaces that person's monogram.
- Motif: the animated tide lines (a nod to the Bay of Fundy), generated by `tides()` in `common.py`.

## Voice and writing rules

- Warm, plain-spoken, practical and confident. Short sentences. Lead with the reader's problem, then the outcome.
- Canadian spelling (labour, centre, organization).
- **No em dashes.** Use commas, periods or colons.
- Describe Stephanie as a senior HR leader or senior-level HR consultant. Do not call her an "executive" in her own description.
- Write for CEOs and business owners, mostly in Atlantic Canada, with North America as the wider market.
- Avoid jargon, hype and stock phrases. Never promise results.

## Content standards

- Every statistic needs a named, linkable source, and the article lists it under `sources`. Prefer original research (Gartner, LHH, Conference Board, APEC, Statistics Canada, Microsoft Work Trend Index) over aggregators. Flag any statistic older than 12 months during health checks.
- Paraphrase sources. Quote at most one short phrase per source, under 15 words.
- Never name a client, employer engagement or individual's results without written permission. Use anonymized descriptions ("a high-growth technology company").
- Do not mention Stephanie's departure from any employer, health matters or anything personal.
- Collective partner bios change only with that partner's approval.
- Keep `llms.txt`, `sitemap.xml` and the JSON-LD schema accurate when pages or services change (they are generated by the build).

## Recurring jobs

- **Weekly thought leadership scan:** research new, credible sources on HR, leadership, succession, restructuring, AI at work and Atlantic Canadian business. Deliver a briefing with 3 article ideas (headline, angle, key sources) and 2 LinkedIn post drafts in Stephanie's voice. Do not open a PR unless asked.
- **Article drafting:** on request, write the article as a Markdown file in `content/insights/` and open a PR.
- **Monthly health check:** build, run the link check, verify external source links still resolve, flag statistics older than 12 months, and open one PR with fixes.
